{\huge Footnotes to the Network Monitoring Report:\ {\bf \obsband}\ \expcode}
\smallskip\hrule\hrule\vspace{1cm}\parindent=0pt\parskip=4pt

{\bf General:}

{\bf a) Jb2, Jodrell Bank Mark II:} Fringes found only on the disk correlation. The signal is significantly weaker than expected (by a factor of $\sim 1/3$). Pointing errors could explain this, but no feedback unveiling the origin of the issue has been provided before the submission of this report.

{\bf b) Mc, Medicina:} Lost of the first scan due to a system restart.

{\bf c) Ur, Urumqi:} Could not observe because the antenna gearbox has malfunctioned before the session and it is still under maintenance.

{\bf d) Tr, Toru\`n:} Recorded with swapped polarizations (fixed during correlation).

{\bf e) Mh, Metsah\"ovi:} Issues on the synchronization of the data transferred during the FTP test (and fixed after restarting).

{\bf f) KVN:} The a-priori rates derived from the sent GPS clock were significantly off (but well within limits to fringe fit the data). We noticed that when such clocks are stored in the local system (both into the filesystem logs and to the EVN monitor), the values are significantly rounded, causing a step-function profile along the time.
